This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Modern Relationship Intelligence Tools**. ## Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global sales and revenue intelligence market is estimated at approximately **$5.37 billion in 2026** (with the broader personal & relationship CRM market reaching **$16.5 billion**), expanding at a double-digit CAGR. While historically **moderately fragmented** into niche point solutions across VC dealflow, legal networks, and meeting transcription, the space is rapidly **consolidating** toward unified platforms as enterprise CRM giants and revenue orchestration leaders integrate native AI relationship graphs.

### Additional Infrastructure & Building Blocks

- **Email Parsing & Activity Capture**: [Mailparser](https://github.com/nodemailer/mailparser), [ImapFlow](https://github.com/postalsys/imapflow) for extracting interaction data from email streams.
- **Calendar Integration**: CalDAV, iCalendar, and Nylas SDKs for tracking meeting dynamics and warm intros.
- **Identity & Record Matching**: Dedupe, Splink, and Zingg for multi-source contact deduplication.
- **Graph Machine Learning**: PyTorch Geometric and DGL for link prediction, warm intro scoring, and contact recommendation.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Neo4j** or **NebulaGraph** for the relationship graph, **NetworkX** for analysis, **Cytoscape.js** or **Sigma.js** for visualization, **SuiteCRM** or **Twenty** for CRM data, and **Apache Superset** for dashboards to build a complete self-hosted relationship intelligence stack.
